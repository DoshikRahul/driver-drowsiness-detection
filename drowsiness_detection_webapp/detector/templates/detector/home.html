How It Works

Facial-landmark geometry fused with expression scores. No special hardware, just a camera.

1. Capture

Use your webcam for continuous monitoring, or upload a single photo of the driver's face.

2. Measure

MediaPipe locates 478 face landmarks. We compute the Eye Aspect Ratio, Mouth Aspect Ratio and head pitch.

3. Alert

If the eyes stay closed or the head droops for too long, an audible alarm sounds and the event is logged.
