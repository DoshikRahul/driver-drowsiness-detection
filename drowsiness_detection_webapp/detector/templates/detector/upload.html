Tips for best results

Face the camera
A frontal or slightly-angled face works best. Strong profile views reduce accuracy.
Good lighting
Avoid heavy shadows or backlight over the eyes.
Sunglasses
Dark glasses hide the eyes; eye closure can't be measured through them.
Use live mode
A single photo can't tell a blink from sleep. Live mode measures how long the eyes stay closed.
